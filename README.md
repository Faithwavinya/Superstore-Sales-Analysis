Data Analyst Portfolio Project
1. Problem Statement
While analyzing a retail dataset with $2.2M in sales volume, I identified a significant "margin leakage" within specific high-revenue categories. Despite strong sales, the Furniture sector was consistently underperforming in net profit compared to other departments. The existing data lacked a clear visualization to explain why high transaction volumes were failing to translate into take-home profit.

2. Business Objective
My objective for this project was to engineer a diagnostic tool to help stakeholders:

Identify sub-categories where the Average Discount is cannibalizing the Profit Margin.

Pinpoint geographic Regions where discounting strategies are misaligned with sales growth.

Provide a data-driven foundation for a more profitable "Discount Floor" pricing policy.

3. My Approach
I took full ownership of the end-to-end analytical process:

Data Synthesis: Cleaned and aggregated raw transaction records into a structured Pivot Table architecture.

Metric Engineering: Calculated the "Break-even" point by comparing Sum of Profit against Average Discount rates.

User Experience: Developed an interactive "Drill-Down" system using Slicers for real-time comparative analysis by Region and Category.

Risk Visualization: Implemented automated KPI indicators (icons) to highlight "Red Zone" losses for immediate executive review.

4. Tools and Techniques Used

Microsoft Excel: Pivot Tables, Slicers, Timeline Filters.

Data Visualization: Combo Charts (Dual-Axis), KPI Icon Sets, Dashboard Design.

Analytical Techniques: Correlation Analysis, Margin Variance Analysis, Retail Pricing Logic.

5. Key Insights

The "Table" Trap: Tables are the 3rd highest revenue driver ($206k), yet they are the most significant source of net loss for the company.

The Critical Threshold: My analysis showed a direct inverse correlation: once the average discount exceeds 22%, the sub-category consistently falls into a Net Loss.

High-Efficiency Categories: The Technology category maintains a $145k profit margin by keeping discounts disciplined at 13%, proving high volume does not require deep discounting.

6. Business Recommendations

Implement a Discount Cap: I recommend capping Furniture discounts at 15% to shift "Tables" from a loss back to a positive margin.

Regional Strategy Alignment: The Central Region requires a targeted audit to align their discounting tactics with the more profitable West region.
